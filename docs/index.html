How to play
  • Press Play to start. The clock starts then and keeps running, even if you leave the page.
  • Guess any common word. Each guess gets a rank out of about 31,000: the answer is #1, and the lower the rank, the closer you are.
  • Plurals and verb forms count as their base word. Names, places and brands aren't accepted.

Clues and Hints

  • Clues are three written clues, from a broad category to close to a definition. They cost 5, 10 and 15 points.
  • Hints reveal a real word near the answer: Hint 1 one ranked #6–10, Hint 2 one ranked #2–5, always closer than your best. They cost 20 and 25 points.
  • Help unlocks only when you're stuck, in order: Clue 1, 2, 3, then Hint 1, 2. The first needs 10 guesses, and each one needs 5 guesses in a row without a new best. A puzzle without clues goes straight to Hints.

Scoring

  • A solve starts at 100. Time costs nothing for the first 2 minutes, then a little more each minute, up to 30 points. Clues and Hints cost what's shown on them. After 15 guesses, every 3 more cost 1 point, up to 15.
  • A solve never scores below 25.
  • Quit reveals the answer and scores at most 20: a share of what you'd have scored, more the closer your best guess was (nothing if it was ranked #1,000 or worse). Solving always beats quitting.

The clock starts when you press Play and keeps running until you solve or quit.
